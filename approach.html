Our approach

AI-native services that get you to strategy in two weeks

We pair agents that read, map and analyse a company at a scale no consulting team can match with a forward-deployed team that does the interviews, the site walks and the heavy lifting agents cannot. Every engagement starts with the same standard implementation, which lands in two weeks, and continues with a subscription that keeps the work in practice and measures what it delivers.

The two-week implementation

A standard implementation, the same for every company

Because the implementation is standard, you know before we start what you will have at the end of the two weeks, what it costs and how much of management's time it needs.

Days 1 to 2
01 · Days 1 to 2

Focus and access

We agree the questions that matter most to the deal and how success will be measured, open access to the documents and the first systems, and book time with the leadership team and the people who run the key processes.

You get: the questions we will answer, an access plan and an interview calendar.

02 · Days 3 to 5

Capture

Agents read the documents, the system exports and the meeting notes. Our team runs the interviews and the site walks. Every new fact carries its source and is reviewed by a person before anyone relies on it.

You get: a searchable picture of the business that grows every day of the implementation.

03 · Days 6 to 8

Diagnose

We map processes, systems, people, customers, vendors and money flows, rank the gaps, inefficiencies, risks and opportunities by size, confidence and the effort it takes to act, and test the findings with management.

You get: an operating map, a ranked list of opportunities and a risk register, every item sourced.

04 · Days 9 to 10

Readout

We walk the sponsor and the leadership team through what we found and agree the first priorities for the hundred-day plan, with owners, and with the evidence behind each one.

You get: the strategy readout, the first priorities and live access for the sponsor and management.

05 · After week two

Subscription

The work continues on a weekly, monthly and quarterly rhythm, so the picture stays current, the business uses it in practice and its effectiveness is measured against the outcomes you set.

You get: impact you can see and measure for as long as you own the company.

At the end of week two

What the sponsor and the leadership team have on the last day

  • An operating picture of the business, function by function, with the evidence behind every fact
  • Gaps, inefficiencies, risks and opportunities, sized and ranked
  • The first priorities for the hundred-day plan, each with an owner
  • Answers to the questions you set on the first day, with their sources
  • Live access for the sponsor and management, at the level each role calls for
  • A subscription proposal that names the outcomes we will measure

The subscription

We stay with the business, keep the work in practice and measure its effectiveness

A business keeps changing after the first readout, and so does what an owner needs to know. The subscription is priced per company per month and runs on a steady rhythm, so the impact is measured instead of assumed.

Every week

Kept current

Every meeting, document and system change that matters is captured and reviewed, so the picture never goes stale.

Every month

Put into practice

We review how leadership and teams use it, which decisions and processes run on it and where adoption lags, and we help close the gaps.

Every quarter

Measured for effectiveness

We score progress against the outcomes agreed at the start, from time to answer to initiatives delivered and KPI movement, and report it to the sponsor.

On demand

Answers and readouts

Agents prepare recurring readouts and board materials and answer the sponsor's questions, drawing on the same evidence.

Agents and people

Agents do the reading, and our people do the heavy lift

Some of the most important knowledge in a company is never written down. Our team goes and gets it, so what you see reflects how the business really runs rather than how its documents say it runs.

Interviews

Structured conversations with leaders and the people on the floor, recorded and transcribed with consent.

Data discovery

We find where the data lives, pull it safely and make sense of legacy systems nobody has documented in years.

System connections

We connect to the ERP, CRM, warehouse, HR and finance systems the company runs on, with the access it agrees to.

Site walks

We walk the floor, the warehouse and the service desk, because the gap between the process map and the process lives there.

Each implementation is led by an engagement lead who owns the questions, the relationship with the deal team and management, and the readout, working with a forward-deployed engineer who connects the systems and the data and an interview and research lead who makes sure what people know is captured. Agents do the reading, cross-referencing and drafting that would otherwise take a much larger team.

Trust and security

Built for boards, buyers and auditors to rely on

Operating knowledge is sensitive, and pre-close information more so. We treat it that way from the first document to the last export.

Access by role

Team, management and leadership levels apply to every fact and every query, through every interface.

A full history

Every change records who proposed it, who approved it and the source it cites. Nothing is overwritten silently.

Secrets kept out

Credentials are screened out before anything is recorded, and systems are referenced by name and role.

One instance per company

Each company runs in its own instance, in our cloud or in yours, so data never mixes across the portfolio.

Yours to keep

Everything exports in open formats at any time and transfers with the company at exit.

Clean-team ready

Pre-close work can run under the clean-team rules your counsel sets, with access limited to the people they name.

Works with what you already use

Connected to the company's systems and to your AI tools

Underneath every engagement is a Business Brain: a living, cited picture of the company that people and agents can query. It connects to the systems the business runs on and to the AI assistants and agents your teams already use through MCP and API, with every access rule intact. We are independent of any one AI model, so the work improves as the models do.

Questions we hear

What owners ask before they start

What do we have at the end of the two weeks?

An operating picture of the business with the evidence behind every fact, the gaps, risks and opportunities sized and ranked, the first priorities for the hundred-day plan with owners, answers to the questions you set on the first day, and live access for the sponsor and management.

How much management time does it take?

About an hour with each senior leader, shorter conversations with the people who run key processes, and a thirty-minute check-in at the end of each week. Agents and our team do the rest.

What happens after the two weeks?

The subscription keeps the work current, reviews how the business uses it in practice and measures its effectiveness every quarter against the outcomes you set. If you stop, everything we built stays with the company.

How is it priced?

The two-week implementation is a fixed fee agreed before we start. The subscription is priced per company per month, and a sponsor view across several companies is priced for the fund.

Who owns what you build?

The company does. Each company has its own instance, everything exports in open formats at any time, and it transfers with the company when you sell.

Which AI models do you use?

We are independent of any one model. Each task goes to the model that suits it, and the work connects to the AI tools your teams already use through MCP, so you are never locked in.

How do you handle confidential and pre-close information?

Every fact carries an access level from team to leadership, every change is logged with its author and approver, credentials are screened out, and nothing counts without review. Pre-close work can run under the clean-team rules your counsel sets.

Does this replace our consultants and advisers?

It changes what you need them for. We do the capture and analysis that usually fills the first weeks of an engagement, so advisers can spend their time on judgment, negotiation and change, working from the same evidence.

What do you need from us to start?

A sponsor at the firm, a sponsor in the company's leadership team, access to the documents and the first systems, and time on the leaders' calendars in the first week.
