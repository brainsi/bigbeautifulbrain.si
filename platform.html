The platform

The Business Brain and the agents that work on it

Every engagement runs on the same platform. The brain holds the company as a living knowledge graph with every fact cited and reviewed, and an agent runtime works on it every day for the people who own and run the business.

Anatomy of a Business Brain

Eight layers of the company, connected the way the business is

Each brain starts with the same structure, so a sponsor can read any company the same way, and grows the detail that makes each business different.

CompanyStrategy, leadership, decisions, meetings and the actions that come out of them
CustomersAccounts, contracts, service levels, revenue and the risks attached to each
Product developmentProducts, systems, roadmaps and the technology they depend on
VendorsSuppliers, contacts, terms and the dependencies they create
PartnersChannels, alliances and the relationships that bring in business
Go-to-marketPositioning, pipeline, pricing, campaigns and opportunities
OperationsProcesses, facilities, systems, risks, incidents and metrics
PeopleTeams, roles, capacity, incentives and key-person dependencies
Sources

Documents, transcripts, emails, system exports and interviews are kept verbatim as the evidence layer.

Proposals

Agents and people propose new facts and changes, each with the source it rests on.

Review

A person approves or rejects each proposal, and every decision is committed to the history.

The graph

Approved facts become linked notes and relationships that agents and people can search and traverse.

The agent runtime

A runtime where agents are spun up, routed and put to work

The brain gives agents the context of the business. The runtime gives them a place to run, the right model for each task, the methods to follow and safe connections to the systems they need.

Runners

Agents run on schedules and on events: a new document, a weekly meeting, a month-end close or a change in a connected system.

Routers

Each task goes to the model and tools that suit it, balancing quality, speed, cost and where the data is allowed to go.

Models

Frontier and open models, chosen per task. The brain stays independent of any one model, so it improves as the models do.

Skills

Packaged methods: interview synthesis, process discovery, Six Sigma projects, board reporting, diligence checklists and your own playbooks.

Connections

Governed links to the systems a company runs on, from ERP and CRM to warehouse, HR, finance, email and documents.

The review loop

Agents write back to the brain only as proposals, so the record improves without anyone losing control of it.

MCP, RAG and API

The brain reaches every AI and every system, and the sponsor reaches the brain

A brain is only useful if people and tools can reach it. Ours exposes the same governed knowledge three ways, and every one of them respects the access level of the person or agent asking.

  • MCP connects the brain to the AI assistants and agents your teams already use, as a named server they can query and propose to.
  • RAG grounds answers in the brain's cited notes, so a response comes with the documents and meetings behind it.
  • API feeds dashboards, data warehouses and the sponsor's own systems.

Governance and security

Built for boards, buyers and auditors to rely on

Operating knowledge is sensitive. The platform treats it that way from the first document to the last export.

Access by note

Team, management and leadership levels apply to every note and every query, through every interface.

A full history

Every change is versioned with who proposed it, who approved it and the source it cites. Nothing is overwritten silently.

Secrets kept out

Proposals are scanned for credentials before they can enter the brain, and systems are referenced by name and role.

One instance per company

Each company's brain runs in its own instance, in our cloud or in yours, so data never mixes across the portfolio.

Yours to keep

The vault and the graph export in open formats at any time and transfer with the company at exit.

Clean-team ready

Pre-close work can run under the clean-team rules your counsel sets, with access limited to the people they name.
