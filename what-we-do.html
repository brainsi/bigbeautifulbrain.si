What we do

We work where the deal needs the understanding most

Private equity firms and corporate acquirers bring us in at the moments when knowing how a business really works decides the outcome: before signing, in the first hundred days, through the hold, with every add-on and in the run-up to a sale.

Diligence Brain · 2 to 3 weeks before signing

See the operating reality before you sign

We load the data room, the management presentation, expert calls and anything the target can share into a Diligence Brain, then map how the company actually delivers its revenue. The deal team gets a cited view of where the investment thesis is supported by evidence, where it rests on assumption, and what to ask management before the price is set.

  • Operating map of processes, systems, vendors and key people
  • Risk register in which every item cites its source
  • Value creation hypotheses ranked by size and confidence
  • Questions for management and a list of what the data room does not show

100-Day Brain · 8 to 12 weeks after closing

Turn the value creation plan into work the company can see

We capture every layer of the business, interview leaders and the people who do the work, and discover how orders, money, products and decisions actually flow. Gaps, inefficiencies and quick wins come out sized and ranked, and the value creation plan moves into the brain with owners, dates and the evidence behind each initiative, so progress is visible to the board and the sponsor every week.

  • Full capture across all eight layers of the company
  • Process discovery with the bottlenecks and single points of failure marked
  • A 100-day plan with owners, milestones and weekly readouts
  • Quick wins delivered inside the first hundred days

Operating Brain · through the hold

Run the company from one source of truth

After the first hundred days the brain becomes the operating memory of the business, on subscription. Agents keep it current from meetings, documents and systems and answer leadership's questions with their sources, we keep reviewing how it is implemented and used in practice across the company and measure its effectiveness every quarter, and it runs the disciplines the operator chooses across strategy, reporting, process, finance, people, product, service delivery and customer experience.

  • Leadership reporting and BI built on cited facts
  • Process optimization with excellence benchmarking
  • Cash, cost and working-capital visibility
  • Customer, NPS and service-delivery tracking

Growth Brain · 4 to 6 weeks per add-on

Add acquisitions without losing the thread

In a buy-and-build, every add-on is captured into the platform company's brain, so integration, synergies, cross-sell and pricing decisions are made against one picture of the combined business. The same brain supports the sales strategy, the pricing work and the commercial plan that the growth case depends on.

  • Add-on capture mapped onto the platform's structure
  • Integration plans and synergy tracking with sources
  • Sales strategy, pricing and cross-sell analysis
  • One view of customers, vendors and systems across the group

Exit Brain · 6 to 10 weeks before launch

Sell with the evidence already organized

By the time you prepare a sale, the brain holds the history of the hold: what changed, what it delivered and the facts behind every number. The equity story, vendor diligence and the data room come together from evidence that already exists, and the buyer inherits a brain that keeps running after closing.

  • An equity story backed by cited facts and KPI history
  • Vendor diligence support and a structured data room
  • Documented processes and a clean view of key-person risk
  • A brain the buyer can keep using from day one

Portfolio Brain · across the fund

See every company the same way

When several portfolio companies run a brain, the sponsor can read them through one interface. Operating partners compare processes, cost structures and initiatives across companies, share the playbooks that work, and connect their own AI tools to every brain through MCP, with each company's access rules intact.

  • One sponsor view across portfolio brains
  • Benchmarks across companies in the same vertical
  • Playbooks that move from one company to the next
  • Secure MCP, RAG and API access for the deal and operating teams

The forward-deployed team

Agents do the reading, and our people do the heavy lifting

Some of the most important knowledge in a company is never written down. Our team goes and gets it, so the brain reflects how the business really runs rather than how the documents say it runs.

Interviews

Structured conversations with leaders and the people on the floor, recorded, transcribed and filed into the brain with consent.

Data discovery

We find where the data lives, pull it safely and make sense of legacy systems that nobody has documented in years.

System connections

We connect the brain to the ERP, CRM, warehouse, HR and finance systems the company runs on, with the access it agrees to.

Site walks

We walk the floor, the warehouse and the service desk, because the gap between the process map and the process lives there.
