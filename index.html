What we do

The understanding of a business that usually takes a new owner a year, in the first weeks.

Every acquisition, whether a portfolio company or a restaurant, starts with the same problem. The knowledge that runs it lives in people's heads, in inboxes and in systems that do not talk to each other, and it leaves with the people who hold it.

We capture that knowledge once, into a Business Brain in which every fact traces back to its source, and keep agents working on it every day. You decide with evidence within weeks, and what the business knows grows instead of resetting with every new hire, adviser or owner.

A working picture in two weeks

How the business really earns its money, function by function and location by location, with every fact traced to the document, meeting or system it came from.

Value found while you can act

Gaps, leaks, risks and opportunities, sized and ranked in the first weeks of ownership rather than the first year.

Agents that keep it working

Persistent agents follow the journeys that carry the value, keep the picture current and report what changed. We measure what they deliver every quarter.

Two industries, one arc

Private equity firms and restaurant groups make the same five moves

Both buy businesses they did not build, need to understand them fast, run them leaner, grow or prune them, and sell. The vocabulary changes; the problem does not.

The AINS approach

AI-native services, with persistent agents on the journeys that carry the value

An AI-native services firm sells outcomes, not software seats. A small senior team and a swarm of agents do the work, and we are measured on what it delivers.

Our agents do not answer once and forget. Each one stays with a journey for weeks or months: it remembers every step, works from the cited brain, wakes when something changes, and hands to a person when judgment is needed.

An add-on, from the first conversation with its founder to a business absorbed into the platform

One agent holds the thread for well over a year, across the deal team, the operating partner and management.

Agent worksA person decides
  1. Months beforeThe relationship stays warm

    Every call, letter and signal about the target is kept, and the deal partner is prompted when it is time to reach out.

  2. Week 1 of exclusivityThe data room becomes a brain

    Operations, customers, contracts and people mapped, with the questions for management ranked.

  3. Week 3The integration plan is approved

    The operating partner signs off owners, dates and the synergies that matter.

  4. Day 1 after closingMapped onto the platform

    Customers, vendors, systems and roles placed against the platform's own, overlaps flagged.

  5. Weeks 2 to 12Synergies tracked weekly

    Progress against plan, drift flagged early, the weekly readout drafted.

  6. Quarter endThe board sees what it delivered

    The CFO reviews the numbers, each traced to its source, before they go to the board.

Nothing is relearned when the deal team hands to the operating team.Every step is recorded with its source.

A corporate dinner for eighty, from the first inquiry to next year's booking

One agent holds the thread for ten months, across the events team, the kitchen, the floor and the client.

Agent worksA person decides
  1. MinutesThe inquiry is answered

    Dates, rooms, menus and minimums that fit the request, from what the group actually offers.

  2. Day 1The proposal is approved

    The events manager adjusts the menu and the price, and the deposit link goes out.

  3. Weeks 2 to 6Every detail followed up

    Tasting, headcount, dietary needs and timings chased, and the kitchen and floor briefed.

  4. The nightThe team runs the event

    The GM and chef work from one brief, with the client's preferences on it.

  5. Day 2Thanks, feedback, invoice

    The balance settled, the feedback filed against the menu, the room and the team.

  6. Month 10Next year is offered first

    The client hears from you with the same date and what they liked, before anyone else calls.

No inquiry waits overnight.No account goes quiet between events.Every step is recorded with its source.
