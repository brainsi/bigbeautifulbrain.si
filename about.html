About us

New owners should not need a year to understand the business they bought

Big Beautiful Brain is an AI-native services company. We work with private equity firms and corporate acquirers to get their heads and hands around the businesses they buy in two weeks, and to keep that understanding in practice, and measured, through the hold and into the exit.

Why we exist

Every change of ownership slows a business down

When a business changes hands, the people who know how it works are asked the same questions by the deal team, the advisers, the new board and the consultants who follow, and the strategy waits while everyone catches up.

We started Big Beautiful Brain after our first implementation inside a multi-site distribution business, where leadership had a cited, reviewed picture of the company within nine days and could see the risks and opportunities worth acting on. Owners make better decisions sooner when the knowledge of a business is captured once, checked by the people who run it and kept working through the hold, and we built a services company to make that the normal way to own a business.

What we hold to

Six principles behind every engagement

Evidence over opinion

Every finding cites the document, meeting or system it came from, so a claim can always be checked.

People stay in control

Agents propose and people approve. Nothing counts without a review, and every change is logged.

Access by design

Sensitive knowledge is protected fact by fact, so the floor, management and leadership each see what they should.

It belongs to the business

Each company has its own instance and can export it at any time. It goes with the company when the company is sold.

Agents and people together

Agents bring scale and speed. Our team brings the interviews, the access and the judgment that agents cannot supply.

Measured every quarter

We hold ourselves to the outcomes you set at the start, and we measure them with you every quarter.
