What our approach leads to

Strategy in two weeks, and impact you can measure every quarter after that.

Every acquisition starts with the same race. The knowledge that runs the company lives in people's heads, in inboxes and in systems that do not talk to each other, and until a new owner understands it, the plan waits.

We pair agents that read, map and analyse a company at a scale no consulting team can match with a forward-deployed team that does the interviews, the site walks and the heavy lifting. Together they bring the time from kickoff to strategy down to two weeks, and they keep the work accountable long after the first readout.

The two-week implementation

Ten working days from kickoff to a strategy you can act on

Every engagement starts with the same standard implementation, so you know before we begin what you will have at the end of the two weeks, what it costs and how much of management's time it takes.

Days 1 to 2
01 · Days 1 to 2

Focus and access

We agree the questions that matter most to the deal, open access to the documents and the first systems, and book time with the leadership team and the people who run the key processes.

02 · Days 3 to 5

The whole business, captured

Agents read everything that is written down while our team interviews leaders and the people on the floor and walks the operation, so the picture reflects how the company really runs rather than how its documents say it runs.

03 · Days 6 to 8

Where value is won and lost

We map how orders, money, products and decisions flow through the business, size and rank the gaps, inefficiencies, risks and opportunities, and check the findings with management before anyone relies on them.

04 · Days 9 to 10

Strategy and first priorities

The sponsor and the leadership team get the readout: the operating picture, the ranked opportunities and the first priorities for the hundred-day plan, each with the evidence behind it, and live access to everything we found.

05 · After week two

In practice, and measured

Our subscription keeps the picture current as the business changes, reviews how leadership and teams are using it in practice, and measures its effectiveness every quarter against the outcomes you set.

The subscription

After the first two weeks, we stay and measure the impact

Understanding a business once is not enough, because the business keeps changing. Our subscription keeps the work current, in use and accountable for as long as you own the company.

It connects to the systems and the AI tools the company already uses, so the leadership team and the sponsor read the same picture, and it stays with the business when you sell.

How the subscription works
Every week

Kept current

Every meeting, document and system change that matters is captured and reviewed, so the picture never goes stale.

Every month

Put into practice

We review how leadership and teams use it, which decisions and processes run on it and where adoption lags, and we help close the gaps.

Every quarter

Measured for effectiveness

We score progress against the outcomes agreed at the start, from time to answer to initiatives delivered and KPI movement, and report it to the sponsor.

Evidence you can rely on

Findings you can put in front of a board

Every finding has a source

Each fact links to the document, meeting or system it came from, so anyone can check it.

People approve every change

Agents propose and a person reviews before anything counts, and every change is logged.

Access follows the org chart

Team, management and leadership levels decide who sees what, down to the individual fact.

It stays with the company

Each company has its own instance. It exports at any time and goes with the business when you sell.
