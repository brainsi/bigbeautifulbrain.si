About us

A business should not forget what it knows when its owners change

Big Beautiful Brain is an AI-native services company. We work with private equity firms, corporate acquirers and restaurant and hospitality groups to capture how the companies and concepts they acquire really work, and we keep that knowledge working for them, with persistent agents, from the first look at a target to the next transaction.

Why we exist

Every change of ownership puts a company's knowledge at risk

When a business changes hands, the people who know how it works are asked the same questions by the deal team, the advisers, the new board and the consultants who follow, and each group leaves with its own partial picture. In a restaurant the same knowledge sits with the chef, the general manager and the bar lead, and it leaves when they do.

We started Big Beautiful Brain after building the first Business Brain inside a multi-site distribution business and seeing what changed when its leadership could look at the whole company in one place, with every fact traced to its source, within days rather than months. Owners make better decisions sooner when the knowledge of the business is captured once, reviewed by the people who run it and kept working through the hold, and we built a services company around making that the normal way to own a business.

What we hold to

Six principles behind every brain we build

Evidence over opinion

Every fact in a brain cites the document, meeting or system it came from, so a claim can always be checked.

People stay in control

Agents propose and people approve. Nothing becomes part of the record without a review, and every change is logged.

Access by design

Sensitive knowledge is protected note by note, so the floor, management and leadership each see what they should.

The brain belongs to the business

Each company has its own instance and can export it at any time. It transfers with the company in any transaction.

Agents and people together

Agents bring scale and speed. Our team brings the interviews, the access and the judgment that agents cannot supply.

Built to compound

A brain should be worth more every month it runs, because every meeting, document and decision makes it richer.
