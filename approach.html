Our approach

AI-native services, with agents that stay with the work

We sell outcomes rather than software: a small senior team and a swarm of agents do the work, and we are measured on what it delivers. Underneath every engagement is the same foundation, a Business Brain that holds the company as a cited, living knowledge graph, and persistent agents that work on it every day, on the journeys that carry the value.

AI-native services

Outcomes, not software seats

Owners have two familiar options. Advisers bring judgment and leave a report that starts to age the day it is delivered. Software scales, but someone has to adopt it, configure it and feed it the context it lacks. An AI-native services firm, an AINS, delivers the outcome itself.

Advisers

Judgment, then a document

Senior people for a few weeks, priced by their time. The understanding they build leaves with them, and the next adviser starts again.

Software

Capability without context

Powerful tools that each know one task and none of the business. Your team does the adopting, the configuring and the chasing.

An AI-native services firm

The outcome, delivered and measured

Agents do the reading, the reconciling and the follow-through; our people do the interviews and the judgment. The knowledge stays in a brain the business owns, and we measure what it delivers every quarter.

Persistent agents

Agents that stay with a journey for months, not a single question

Most AI at work answers a question and forgets it. The journeys that create value in a business run for weeks, months or years: an acquisition, a key account, a sale, an event booked a year ahead, a regular guest.

A persistent agent stays with one of those journeys from beginning to end. It keeps the whole history in memory, works from the cited brain, wakes when something happens, does the follow-through people forget, and hands to a person wherever judgment or a relationship calls for one.

  1. Wakes on an email, a booking, a date or a change in a system
  2. Remembers every step, person and promise in the journey
  3. Reads the brain for cited facts about the business
  4. Acts: drafts, chases, briefs and updates
  5. Asks a person wherever your rules say it should
  6. Records each step, with its source, then waits for the next event

The Business Brain

What the agents work from: the business, connected the way it really works

Each brain starts with the same structure, so an owner can read any company or group the same way, and grows the detail that makes each business different.

CompanyStrategy, leadership, decisions, meetings and the actions that come out of them
Customers and guestsAccounts, contracts, regulars, events, revenue and the risks attached to each
Products and menusProducts, menus, recipes, systems and the technology they depend on
VendorsSuppliers, terms, prices and the dependencies they create
PartnersChannels, landlords, alliances and the relationships that bring in business
Go-to-marketPositioning, pipeline, pricing, campaigns and opportunities
OperationsProcesses, locations, facilities, systems, risks, incidents and metrics
PeopleTeams, roles, capacity, incentives and key-person dependencies
Sources

Documents, transcripts, emails, system exports and interviews are kept verbatim as the evidence layer.

Proposals

Agents and people propose new facts and changes, each with the source it rests on.

Review

A person approves or rejects each proposal, and every decision is committed to the history.

The graph

Approved facts become linked notes and relationships that agents and people can search and traverse.

The runtime

Where the agents run, and how they reach your systems

The brain gives agents the context of the business. The runtime gives them a place to run for as long as a journey lasts, the right model for each task, the methods to follow and governed connections to the systems the business runs on.

The same knowledge is reachable three ways, each respecting the access level of the person or agent asking: MCP for the AI assistants and agents your teams already use, RAG for answers grounded in cited notes, and an API for dashboards and the owner's own systems.

Runners

Agents run on schedules and on events: a new document, a weekly meeting, a month-end close or a change in a connected system.

Routers

Each task goes to the model and tools that suit it, balancing quality, speed, cost and where the data is allowed to go.

Models

Frontier and open models, chosen per task. The brain stays independent of any one model, so it improves as the models do.

Skills

Packaged methods: interview synthesis, process discovery, Six Sigma projects, board reporting, diligence checklists and your own playbooks.

Connections

Governed links to the systems a company runs on, from POS, ERP and CRM to scheduling, HR, finance, email and documents.

The review loop

Agents write back to the brain only as proposals, so the record improves without anyone losing control of it.

Governance and security

Built for boards, partners, lenders and buyers to rely on

Operating knowledge is sensitive. The platform treats it that way from the first document to the last export.

Access by note

Team, management and leadership levels apply to every note and every query, through every interface.

A full history

Every change is versioned with who proposed it, who approved it and the source it cites. Nothing is overwritten silently.

Secrets kept out

Proposals are scanned for credentials before they can enter the brain, and systems are referenced by name and role.

One instance per company

Each company or group runs its own instance, in our cloud or in yours, so data never mixes across a portfolio.

Yours to keep

The vault and the graph export in open formats at any time and transfer with the business in any transaction.

Clean-team ready

Pre-close work can run under the clean-team rules your counsel sets, with access limited to the people they name.
