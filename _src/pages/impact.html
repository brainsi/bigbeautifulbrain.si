Impact

What changes when you understand the business in two weeks

We work at the moments when understanding a business decides the outcome: before you sign, in the first two weeks after closing, through the first hundred days and the hold, with every add-on and in the run-up to a sale. At each one, our approach shortens the time between a question and a decision you can defend.

Before you sign · two weeks

Know what you are buying before the price is set

We turn the data room, the management presentation, expert calls and anything the target can share into an operating picture of the company, and show the deal team where the investment thesis is supported by evidence, where it rests on assumption, and what to ask management while the answers can still change the price.

  • Where the thesis holds, and where it rests on assumption
  • Risks, each traced to its source
  • Value creation hypotheses ranked by size and confidence
  • What the data room does not show, and who to ask

After closing · the standard two-week implementation

Reach a strategy while the deal is still fresh

In the first two weeks after closing we capture how the company really works, from the leadership team to the floor, and hand the sponsor and management one shared, cited picture of the business: how it makes money, where it leaks value and what to do first. Strategy starts at the end of week two, with most of the first hundred days still ahead.

  • The operating picture, function by function
  • Opportunities and risks, sized and ranked
  • First priorities for the hundred-day plan
  • Live access for the sponsor and the leadership team

The first 100 days

Spend the first hundred days on the right things

With the picture in place, the plan starts from evidence rather than assumption. Each initiative carries an owner, a date and the facts behind it, quick wins land in the first weeks, and the sponsor sees progress every week instead of waiting for the next board meeting.

  • Initiatives with owners, milestones and evidence
  • Quick wins delivered early
  • Weekly progress for the board and the sponsor
  • Drift flagged while it is still easy to correct

Through the hold · the subscription

Run the company on facts, and measure what works

Our subscription keeps the understanding current as the business changes, reviews how leadership and teams use it in practice, and measures its effectiveness every quarter against the outcomes you set, across finance, people, product, service delivery and customer experience.

  • Leadership reporting you can defend
  • Process improvement measured against benchmarks
  • Cash, cost and working-capital visibility
  • A quarterly effectiveness scorecard for the sponsor

Buy and build · two weeks per add-on

Integrate add-ons without losing pace

Every add-on gets its own two-week implementation, connected to the platform company's picture, so integration, synergies, cross-sell and pricing decisions are made against one view of the combined business instead of a stack of spreadsheets.

  • Each add-on understood in two weeks
  • Integration and synergy tracking with evidence
  • Sales strategy, pricing and cross-sell analysis
  • One view of customers, vendors and systems across the group

At exit

Show a buyer how the business works

By the time you prepare a sale, the history of the hold is already organized: what changed, what it delivered and the evidence behind every number. The equity story, vendor diligence and the data room draw on facts that already exist, and a buyer can see how the business runs before it has to ask.

  • An equity story backed by evidence and KPI history
  • Vendor diligence answered from existing facts
  • Documented processes and a clear view of key-person risk
  • A handover the next owner can keep running

Across the portfolio

See every company the same way

When several portfolio companies work with us, the sponsor reads them through one view. Operating partners compare processes, cost structures and initiatives across companies, carry the playbooks that work from one company to the next, and connect their own AI tools with each company's access rules intact.

  • One sponsor view across companies
  • Benchmarks across companies in the same sector
  • Playbooks that travel between companies
  • Secure access for the deal and operating teams
