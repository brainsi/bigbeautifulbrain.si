We give a new owner the understanding of a business that usually takes a year, in the first weeks of the hold.

How an engagement runs

From kickoff to strategy in weeks, not quarters

A swarm of agents does the reading, the mapping and the analysis. Our team does the interviews, the site walks and the heavy lifting that turns access into understanding.

Days 1 to 2
01 · Days 1 to 2

Scope and access

We agree the questions that matter to the deal, set access levels from the floor to the boardroom, connect the systems and documents, and book the interviews with management.

02 · Days 3 to 7

Capture

Agents ingest the documents, the systems and the meeting notes while our team runs the interviews and site walks. Every new fact arrives with its source, and a person reviews it before it counts.

03 · Days 8 to 10

Diagnose and read out

We map processes, systems, people, customers, vendors and money flows, rank the gaps, inefficiencies, risks and opportunities by size, confidence and the effort it takes to act, and walk the sponsor and management through what we found. A standard implementation lands in two weeks.

04 · Weeks 3 to 12

Act

The value creation plan lives in the brain with owners and dates. Agents track progress, flag drift early and prepare the weekly readout for leadership and the sponsor.

05 · Ongoing

Review and measure

On subscription, every meeting, document and decision keeps feeding the brain, we keep reviewing how it is implemented and used in practice across the business, and we measure its effectiveness every quarter, so it grows with the company, absorbs each add-on and arrives at the exit already organized.
