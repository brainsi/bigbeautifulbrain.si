What we do

The understanding of a new investment that usually takes the first year of ownership, in its first weeks.

Every acquisition, whether a platform, an add-on or a concept joining a group, starts with the same problem. The knowledge that runs it lives in people's heads, in inboxes and in systems that do not talk to each other, and it leaves with the people who hold it.

We capture that knowledge once, into a Business Brain in which every fact traces back to its source, and keep agents working on it every day. You decide with evidence within weeks, and what the business knows grows instead of resetting with every new hire, adviser or owner.

A working picture in two weeks

How the business really earns its money, function by function and venue by venue, with every fact traced to the document, meeting or system it came from.

Value found while you can act

Gaps, leaks, risks and opportunities, sized and ranked in the first weeks of ownership rather than the first year.

Agents that keep it working

Persistent agents follow the journeys that carry the value, keep the picture current and report what changed. We measure what they deliver every quarter.
