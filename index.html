What we do

We give a new owner the understanding of a business that usually takes a year, in the first weeks of the hold.

Every acquisition starts with the same problem. The knowledge that runs the company lives in people's heads, in inboxes and in systems that do not talk to each other, and the map a consulting team leaves behind starts to age the day it is delivered.

We capture that knowledge into a Business Brain, a living graph of the company in which every fact traces back to its source, and then we put agents to work on it for your deal team, your operating partners and the leadership team you back.

Discover

Agents read the data room, the documents and the systems while our team runs the interviews, the site walks and the data pulls that no agent can do on its own.

Capture

Everything lands in the Business Brain across eight layers of the company, cited to its source and reviewed by a person before it becomes part of the record.

Operate

Agents work on the brain every day, finding gaps and inefficiencies, tracking the value creation plan and answering leadership's questions through MCP, RAG and API.

The Exit Brain

How an engagement runs

From kickoff to a working brain in weeks, not quarters

A swarm of agents does the reading, the mapping and the analysis. Our team does the interviews, the site walks and the heavy lifting that turns access into understanding.

Week 0
01 · Week 0

Scope and access

We agree the questions that matter to the deal, set access levels from the floor to the boardroom, connect the systems and documents, and plan the interviews with management.

02 · Weeks 1 to 2

Capture

Agents ingest the documents, the systems and the meeting notes while our team runs the interviews and site walks. Every new fact arrives as a proposal with its source, and a person reviews it before it becomes part of the brain.

03 · Weeks 2 to 4

Map and diagnose

We map processes, systems, people, customers, vendors and money flows, then rank the gaps, inefficiencies, risks and opportunities by size, confidence and the effort it takes to act on them.

04 · Weeks 4 to 12

Act

The value creation plan lives in the brain with owners and dates. Agents track progress, flag drift early and prepare the weekly readout for leadership and the sponsor.

05 · Ongoing

Compound

Every meeting, document and decision feeds the brain, so it grows with the business, absorbs each add-on and arrives at the exit already organized.

The platform

The brain is a platform, not a report

Underneath every engagement is the same runtime: a Business Brain that holds the company as a living knowledge graph, and an agent layer of runners, routers, models, skills and connections that works on it.

It connects to the systems and AI tools the business already uses, and back to the sponsor through MCP, RAG and API, so the operating partners see the same picture as the leadership team.

How we keep it trustworthy

A brain you can put in front of a board

Every fact cites its source

Each statement in the brain links to the document, meeting or system it came from, so anyone can check it.

People approve what it learns

Agents propose; a person reviews. Nothing becomes part of the record without that review, and every change is logged.

Access follows the org chart

Team, management and leadership levels decide who sees what, down to the individual note.

The brain stays yours

Each company gets its own instance. The vault and the graph can be exported at any time, and they go with the company at exit.
